# Movement catalog references

The health knowledge warehouse was read first. Its `kimwog/items/exercise-dynamic-warmup-same-day-performance-022.md` supports dynamic warmup selection but does not provide a movement library or per-movement dose. The pack therefore cites the following exercise references in `movement_catalog.py`.

- [ACE Exercise Library](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/): exercise names, target areas, and form. Catalog strength alternatives are options, not automatic taxonomy seed rows.
- [ACE activity-specific warmup and mobility guide](https://www.acefitness.org/certifiednewsarticle/2910/a-detailed-guide-to-designing-activity-specific-warm-up-and-mobility-drills/): dynamic preparation and mobility choices.
- [ACE flexibility exercise guide](https://www.acefitness.org/certifiednews/images/article/pdfs/Flexibility.pdf): named static stretches and form.
- [ACSM flexibility guidance](https://www.acsm.org/docs/default-source/publications-files/acsm-resources-exercise-physiologist-download.pdf): 15–30 second flexibility holds. The catalog's 20 seconds sits within that range.

Dynamic repetitions and activation counts are conservative starting defaults for a brief five-minute prep, not claims that a study established an optimal dose for each movement. Pain or restricted range requires individual assessment; the caution tags serve filtering and do not diagnose safety. A movement option should enter the load-bearing program only after its taxonomy muscle map and equipment data exist.

- [NASM exercise library](https://www.nasm.org/workout-exercise-guidance): dumbbell Romanian deadlift, kettlebell deadlift, incline push-up and other strength variants.
- [NASM core programming](https://www.nasm.org/resource-center/blog/training/core-training-programming): Pallof press and Copenhagen plank.
- [NASM functional strength](https://www.nasm.org/resource-center/blog/training/functional-strength-training): landmine press.

- [U.S. Army strength-training basics](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-D101-PURL-gpo76069/pdf/GOVPUB-D101-PURL-gpo76069.pdf): cites ACSM for a general 8–12 repetition starting range.
