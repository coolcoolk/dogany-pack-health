# nutrition/grade-inadequate-position-006

There is a real, influential argument that GRADE mishandles nutrition because it
auto-rates observational diet evidence as low certainty, and a companion claim
that observational data should only be trusted for large effects. But this is a
live dispute: GRADE's own developers reject the "inadequate" label and have
applied GRADE to nutrition guidance, and prominent epidemiologists defend
observational value.

Because it is contested rather than converged, the claim is held at the
observational-equivalent band and carries a contested flag. It should be
presented as one side of an open methodology debate, not as a settled verdict.
