# relationships-communication/source-misattribution-rule-005

Curator rule, source-hygiene case: a specific love-languages validation study
is regularly misattributed online to a different, earlier pair of authors who
are merely cited inside it, not its actual authors. This is a general
corruption vector, not specific to this topic.

Before assigning a grade to any source, confirm the actual authorship and
venue rather than trusting a citation chain at face value.
