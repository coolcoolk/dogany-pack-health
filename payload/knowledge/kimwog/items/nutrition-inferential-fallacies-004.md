# nutrition/inferential-fallacies-004

A nutrition study can be methodologically spotless and still reach an invalid
conclusion, because the failure lives in the inference -- the gap between the
causal question asked, the model fit, and the interpretation drawn. Eight
recurring fallacies drive this, and a grader who only checks design quality will
systematically over-grade nutrition claims.

This is a caution to apply, not a number to cite. When a claim leans on one of
the eight fallacy patterns, it gets downgraded or flagged as contested regardless
of how clean the methods look.
