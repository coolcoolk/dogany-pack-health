# exercise/regional-bias-triceps-longhead-011

Doing triceps extensions with the arm overhead (so the long head of the
triceps is stretched at the top) grows that long head noticeably more than
doing the same exercise with the arm at the side -- about one and a half
times more growth in that specific head over 12 weeks in the study that
tested it directly with MRI.

This is one of the strongest single-study cases for exercise-selection-driven
regional bias currently available, but it is still one trial: treat it as a
solid, not yet independently replicated, basis for programming an overhead
triceps variant when long-head development is a stated focus.
