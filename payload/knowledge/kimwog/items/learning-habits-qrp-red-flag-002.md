# learning-habits/qrp-red-flag-002

Questionable research practices -- flexible analytic choices, post-hoc outlier
exclusion, testing many subsets until something clears significance -- are
still common in the field and are a major driver of the low replication rate
documented elsewhere in this domain.

A lone peer-reviewed finding, absent replication or explicit QRP scrutiny,
should be capped at a C-equivalent confidence level rather than treated as
reliable.
