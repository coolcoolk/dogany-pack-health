# learning-habits/deliberate-practice-ceiling-005

The popularized "10,000 hours" idea overstates what deliberate practice
actually explains. Across a large meta-analysis (157 effect sizes), practice
accounts for only about 12% of the variance in performance -- meaning 88% is
explained by other factors.

The underlying evidence is observational/correlational, not experimental, so
even though the estimate is precise, it caps at a correlational-design
confidence tier, not a causal one.
