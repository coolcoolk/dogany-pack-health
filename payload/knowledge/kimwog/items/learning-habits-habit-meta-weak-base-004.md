# learning-habits/habit-meta-weak-base-004

The habit-formation-duration meta-analysis itself has a weak evidentiary base
underneath it: more than half of its 20 underlying studies were rated high
risk of bias, and most relied on self-report automaticity scales (SRHI/SRBAI)
rather than objective measurement.

The existence of a meta-analysis does not by itself neutralize this -- carry
the caveat whenever citing habit-automaticity numbers that rest on self-report
scales.
