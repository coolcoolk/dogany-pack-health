# mental-health/apa-div12-standard-001

Clinical psychology's real grading anchor is APA Division 12's criteria, now
in its second generation: the current (2015, revised 2023) version requires
systematic reviews of meta-analyses run through a formal PICOTS + committee
GRADE process, replacing the older rule that just two positive RCTs could earn
"well-established" status even when most other trials on the same treatment
were null.

When grading a psychotherapy-efficacy claim in this warehouse, this is the
anchor standard -- and the old two-positive-RCTs shortcut is explicitly the
flaw not to reproduce.
