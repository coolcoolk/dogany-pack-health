# exercise/small-n-culture-006

Typical exercise-science trials are small and underpowered, which is exactly why
the RCT-level band is fragile in this field. A lone positive small-n trial does
not clear the top band and usually should be treated as observational-strength
until an independent replication confirms it.

This is a grading rule that operationalizes the imprecision problem, paired with
the broader replication-crisis finding. Together they define why the
performance-literature lane speaks with more caution than a clinical lane on the
same-sounding claim.
