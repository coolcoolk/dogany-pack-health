# learning-habits/spaced-retrieval-meta-012

Donoghue and Hattie's 2021 meta-analysis pools 242 individual studies (1,619
effect sizes, 169,179 participants total) covering ten learning techniques.
Across all of them, the overall mean effect size is d=0.56. The two strongest
performers are distributed/spaced practice (d=0.85) and practice testing /
retrieval practice (d=0.74) -- the same two techniques the Dunlosky et al.
(2013) consensus review rates HIGH utility (learning-habits/learning-techniques-high-utility-010).

This item exists to attach a quantitative effect-size anchor to that
qualitative HIGH rating: it's a single (if very large) meta-analysis on an
observational-design substrate, which is why it sits at B rather than A.
