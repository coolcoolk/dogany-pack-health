# relationships-communication/love-languages-unsupported-004

The 5 love languages framework is commercially enormous but empirically thin.
Peer-reviewed psychometric work finds the "primary language" concept is
largely a forced-choice-format artifact (people rate all five languages fairly
high on a continuous scale), the five-factor structure doesn't hold up
cleanly, and matching partners' languages predicts satisfaction only weakly --
less than general self-regulation does.

Speak of this as practitioner-level popular psychology, not as an empirically
validated model. Commercial success (20M+ copies sold) is explicitly not
evidence of validity here.
