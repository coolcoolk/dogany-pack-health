# cooking/craft-testing-ceiling-001

The most rigorous craft-testing sources in cooking -- America's Test Kitchen's
blind-tasted recipe iteration (roughly an 80% approval gate) and Serious Eats'
Food Lab controlled experiments -- give recipe-reliability and taste-outcome
claims a genuine but capped confidence tier. This same testing tradition is
what debunked the old "searing seals in the juices" claim -- reverse-sear
methods are measurably juicier despite not searing first.

Taste-outcome claims from this tradition cap at a solid-but-not-top tier;
weaker, untested recipe claims sit much lower. Food-chemistry claims (Maillard
reaction, gelation mechanics, the McGee-style material) are a deliberate
exception and can reach the top tier on their own deterministic grounds.
