# learning-habits/practice-domain-conditional-006

How much deliberate practice matters depends heavily on the domain: using the
corrected 2018 figures, it explains roughly a quarter of variance in games and
music, a fifth in sports, but only about 5% in education and an essentially
non-significant 1% in professional skill.

Always use the 2018-corrected numbers, not the original 2014 figures -- the
original numbers are a documented zombie-statistic hazard that still
circulates uncorrected.

The categories are narrower than their names. "Education" is mostly university
students' course grades. "Professions" is seven effect sizes (321 people) from
programming, military piloting, soccer refereeing and insurance selling, so a
non-significant result there means too few data, not a measured zero. The
original proposer of the deliberate-practice idea also disputes the practice
measure itself (item 005).
