# relationships-communication/couples-therapy-rct-006

Unlike most of this domain, couples therapy (behavioral couples therapy and
emotionally focused couples therapy) has solid RCT-level meta-analytic
support: a medium effect on relationship satisfaction across 33 randomized
trials, and a large pre-post improvement in a broader meta-analysis.

The caveat is sample composition: the meta-analytic evidence base was entirely
opposite-sex couples, so generalization to other couple compositions should
carry an explicit out-of-sample flag.
