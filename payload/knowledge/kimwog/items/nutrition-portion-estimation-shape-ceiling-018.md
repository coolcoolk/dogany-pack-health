# nutrition/portion-estimation-shape-ceiling-018

Someone eyeballing a portion is running an instrument, and the instrument has
been measured. Sixty-seven adults estimated forty-two pre-weighed foods and
drinks in a single supervised session, using either their own hands as a ruler
with geometric formulas, or ordinary cups and spoons.

The hand method won, and winning looked like this: eight in ten geometric foods
landed within a quarter of their true weight, and only about one in eight landed
within a tenth. Cups and spoons managed under three in ten within a quarter. So
the best available unaided technique is a plus-or-minus-twenty-five-percent
instrument, and nothing here supports anyone quoting a portion to the gram.

The part that decides how this gets used is where it broke. The method depends on
the food having a shape you can put a formula to. For the amorphous foods in the
set -- cereal, mashed potato, a muffin, and rice -- not one estimate came within
a tenth of true weight, and exactly one came within a quarter. For irregular
protein pieces, fish fillets and chicken breasts and beef steaks, both methods
were off by more than half.

Read that against a Korean plate. The staple is rice, which is in the failing
category. The item a training consumer most wants counted is a piece of fish or
meat, which is in the worse-than-failing category. The technique works best on
the foods that matter least here.

Three cautions carried deliberately. This was a supervised task with the food
physically in front of the person; an amount recalled at the end of the day is
not better than these numbers and has no measured value at all. This is one
unreplicated study in sixty-seven mostly female Australian adults on
investigator-chosen foods, so the shape-dependence transfers and the percentages
do not. And this error is independent of the two the evidence lane already
records -- what the person fails to report, and what the composition table gets
wrong about correctly reported food. Three separate errors sit between a logged
meal and the truth. They do not cancel, and this item bounds only the middle one.
