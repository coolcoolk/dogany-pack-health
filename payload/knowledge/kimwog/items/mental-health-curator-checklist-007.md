# mental-health/curator-checklist-007

Operating checklist for this lane, distilled from the worked examples above
rather than an independent finding: a top grade needs GRADE-level meta-
analytic support, agreement across at least two grading bodies, real clinical
meaningfulness, and survival of publication-bias adjustment; a solid-but-not-
top grade needs RCT/meta evidence at moderate-or-better certainty against a
meaningful comparator (the CBT-for-GAD item is the template); everything
resting only on passive-control or observational evidence caps lower;
practitioner consensus and anecdote sit at the bottom.

Mandatory downgrade triggers: the effect disappears against an active control,
the effect disappears when restricted to low-risk-of-bias trials, the effect
is significant but not clinically meaningful, the estimate is unadjusted in a
literature with known funnel-plot asymmetry, positive results are implausibly
saturated or preregistration failed as a safeguard, or the proposed mechanism
is implausible despite significant trial results.
