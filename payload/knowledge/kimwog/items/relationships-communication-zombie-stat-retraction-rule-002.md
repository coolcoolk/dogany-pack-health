# relationships-communication/zombie-stat-retraction-rule-002

This is a curator rule, not a primary finding: citation count and popularity
are not evidence of validity. The positivity-ratio figure kept accumulating
citations for eight years after a formal retraction, and one of its original
authors recanted the retraction itself in 2014 -- so "it's widely cited" is
not a defense against a documented refutation.

Treat any claim that has survived a formal retraction as CONTESTED by default,
independent of how often it still gets cited.
