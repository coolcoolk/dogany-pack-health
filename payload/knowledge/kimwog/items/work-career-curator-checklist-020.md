# work-career/curator-checklist-020

This is the operating checklist for grading anything new in this domain,
distilled from the worked examples above rather than a finding in its own
right. It covers both halves of the domain, which do not share a ceiling.

Where the ceilings sit.

In the employment half, the top of the ladder is codified classification and
regulatory fact read at the issuing body -- the burn-out classification, a tax
rule at the tax authority. Below that, meta-analyses and randomised field
trials with no live rebuttal. Below that, single-sample behavioural findings,
laboratory studies run on students, and any predictive coefficient whose
criterion reliability is not stated.

In the independent-work half the ceiling is lower and rarely raised. People
are almost never randomly assigned to start a business -- a randomised
start-up grant lottery in Nigeria is the notable exception -- so the best
design usually available is full-population administrative data, and that is
where the strong band stops unless a randomised design exists. Panel-data econometrics on people who chose self-employment sits a
notch down regardless of the journal.

Triggers that force a downgrade or an unsettled flag:

- a predictive coefficient quoted without saying what it predicts, or what
  reliability was assumed for the thing it predicts
- a personnel-selection number from before the 2022 recalibration, presented
  as current
- any statistic about founders, businesses or careers drawn from a sample of
  people who already succeeded -- this domain's signature failure
- a prevalence figure with no stated cutoff
- a consultancy or vendor report linking a practice to firm performance
- a productivity claim about a work arrangement that omits whether
  participation was voluntary, whether it was full or hybrid, and what the job
  actually was
- a labour-market finding measured in the United States and applied to Korea
  without checking tenure, pay indexation and registration rules
- a personality construct offered as a big lever when the pooled effect is
  small or the construct overlaps one that already exists
- a jurisdiction-bound rate or threshold with no verification date
- a first-person success story offered as evidence for a mechanism

What makes this domain different from the clinical and market lanes in this
warehouse. Trials exist for work arrangements but are rare for founding a
company, so the two halves need separate ceilings. The dominant contaminant is
not commercial funding, it is survivorship -- the people who write careers
advice are, by construction, the ones it worked for. And an unusual share of
the famous numbers here are correct where they were published and wrong where
they are quoted, which is why several triggers above are about the transplant
rather than about the study.
