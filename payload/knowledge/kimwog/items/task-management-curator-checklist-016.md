# task-management/curator-checklist-016

The grading rule for this lane, distilled from its own worked examples.

The top band is reserved for findings with an objective outcome measure at both
ends AND either multi-lab replication or a publication-bias correction the
effect survives. Nothing in this lane's opening set qualifies; that is a
statement about the domain, not about the sprint.

The second band holds large meta-analyses that report a bias correction,
controlled experiments with a clean dose manipulation, and verified negatives
established by reading the primary source. Below that sit correlational
syntheses built on self-report scales and small field observations. At the
bottom, carried but never spoken as measurement: conceptual literature,
published disputes, and searched absences.

Refusal and downgrade triggers, every one of which fired during this lane's
opening sprint:

- A suspiciously precise duration. Twenty-three minutes and fifteen seconds.
  Twenty-five minutes. Twenty-one days. Precision in this domain is a marker of
  transmission, not of measurement.
- A percentage of "productive time". No study in this lane measured a
  productive day, so no study can express a cost as a fraction of one.
- A number attributed to a named study. Check that it is in the study. Three
  times in this sprint it was not.
- An effect size from a meta-analysis whose own authors have since published a
  corrected one. Cite the correction.
- A self-report measure standing in for a behaviour, especially a duration.
- A completion or compliance rate with no independent validator underneath it.
- A named method offered as if the name were the evidence.
- A prescription more specific than anything anyone has measured.

The structural reason this lane needs a harder filter than its neighbours: the
transmission channel is commercial. Claims here are load-bearing in books that
sell, so they circulate with more confidence and less provenance than claims in
a clinical or market domain do. Refusing a famous claim with a stated reason is
the most valuable thing this lane produces.
