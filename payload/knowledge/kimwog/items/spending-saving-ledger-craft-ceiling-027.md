# spending-saving/ledger-craft-ceiling-027

This lane carries the practice of keeping a spending ledger: what an agent does
about a category that has gone quiet, a payee nobody can identify, a day with no
rows, a duplicate that might be real. None of that is in a paper. That is why
the lane exists, and it is also exactly the situation in which a domain
saturated with unsourced rules gets its rules back in through a side door. So
the lane's standard is stated positively, in terms of what an item must rest on.

**The ceiling is an instrument fact.** The strongest thing this lane can say is
a deterministic, checkable property of the recording instrument: that a column
is nullable and unused, that a value set cannot express a transfer, that a load
into a stored-value card is not a purchase. Anyone holding the database can
verify it in one query, and it does not decay. That is a higher ceiling than it
sounds -- higher, in verifiability, than most behavioural results, because it is
not a sample and has nothing to replicate.

**Below it, a census of one ledger.** A real measurement of a real artefact,
whose mechanism travels and whose proportions do not, and which has to say which
is which.

**Below that, a proposed procedure** built on a measured fact, shipped with the
plain statement that no outcome was measured.

Nothing else ships. Four exclusions do the actual work:

1. **Convergence of practitioners is not a basis in this lane**, even though it
   is one next door. The exercise craft lane can triangulate three independently
   published loading schemes and treat their agreement as weak evidence, because
   strength coaching is adjudicated by a body that pushes back: a bad scheme
   produces missed lifts and injuries, so a practice that survived has survived
   something. A badly kept ledger pushes back on nobody. It produces a report
   that looks exactly like a good one -- which is this lane's founding
   observation. In a field with no adjudication loop, practitioners agreeing
   with each other is evidence that they read each other.

2. **No claim about people enters through this lane.** Anything asserting what
   users do, prefer or respond to belongs to the evidence lane and is graded by
   its checklist regardless of subject. This lane's subject is the instrument
   and the operator.

3. **No refused claim re-enters in craft clothing.** The evidence lane refused
   the fixed-ratio budgets, the latte factor and the emergency-fund month counts
   outright, because nothing measured sits underneath them. A ratio restated as
   an envelope discipline, a small-purchase claim restated as an awareness
   habit, a month count restated as a buffer rule -- same claim, new label, same
   refusal. This lane is not a lower gate. It is a different one.

4. **Nothing ships on "it is generally recommended."** If an item cannot name
   the instrument fact, the census, or the identity it rests on, it does not
   ship at any band.

Limit, stated so a later sprint has something specific to argue with: this
standard is distilled from ten worked examples against a single instance ledger.
A second ledger -- with cash habits, a different importer, a different schema --
would very likely force revisions to it.
