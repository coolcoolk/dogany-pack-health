# exercise/funding-coi-check-004

Industry funding by itself does not make a study wrong -- the evidence shows it
does not worsen methodological quality -- but it does skew conclusions toward the
sponsor's product. The correct response is to look for an independent body that
reaches the same finding, not to reflexively downgrade.

Operationally: a funded efficacy claim with no independent corroborator is capped
below the top band and may warrant a contested flag; a funded claim that an
independent institution corroborates can still reach the top. This is the rule
that lets the creatine and protein claims clear their industry-conflict discount.
