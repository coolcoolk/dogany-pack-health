# learning-habits/study-heuristics-low-utility-011

The same APS Dunlosky et al. (2013) review that identifies retrieval practice
and spaced practice as the two strongest techniques (-010) rates several
widely used study habits LOW utility: rereading material, underlining or
highlighting text, and the keyword mnemonic. These are
popular precisely because they feel productive, but the evidence for their
effect on durable learning is weak compared to the HIGH-utility techniques.

This is a practical rebalancing claim, not a "these techniques do nothing"
claim -- the review still finds some conditions where they help, just not
robustly or durably enough to earn a HIGH rating.
