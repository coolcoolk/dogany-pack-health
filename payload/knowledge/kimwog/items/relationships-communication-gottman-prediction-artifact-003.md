# relationships-communication/gottman-prediction-artifact-003

The famous "94% divorce prediction accuracy" headline is a classification
statistic on a low-base-rate sample (only 12.3% of couples actually divorced
over the 4-year window), not a validated prospective predictor. The underlying
correlations (r=-.20 to -.23) are modest, and the reported percentage is
concurrent classification accuracy, not out-of-sample prediction.

Curator rule: any relationship-science claim quoting a bare "% correct" figure
without base rate, cross-validation, and positive predictive value should be
downgraded automatically -- this is the canonical worked example of why.
