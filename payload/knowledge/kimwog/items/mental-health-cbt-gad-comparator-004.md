# mental-health/cbt-gad-comparator-004

CBT for generalized anxiety disorder is one of this domain's stronger claims
versus doing nothing (waitlist/treatment-as-usual), with a Cochrane review and
a large network meta-analysis both showing a moderate-to-strong effect at
moderate certainty.

But the effect shrinks and loses significance against an active comparator
(supportive therapy) in the small head-to-head evidence base, and restricting
to low-risk-of-bias trials erased the apparent benefit of relaxation therapy
specifically. Always specify the comparator when citing this effect, and note
the active-comparator evidence base is still thin (six studies) and could
shift.
