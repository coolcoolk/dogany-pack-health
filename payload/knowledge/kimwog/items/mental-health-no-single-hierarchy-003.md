# mental-health/no-single-hierarchy-003

Unlike a biomedical field with one dominant regulatory body, clinical
psychology's major grading institutions (APA, NICE, Cochrane) frequently
disagree with each other on the same treatments, and RCTs in this field face
structural limits that biomedical RCTs don't (blinding is often impossible,
control conditions are ambiguous).

Practical grading rule: a top-tier claim should require agreement across at
least two independent grading bodies, not just one institution's endorsement.
