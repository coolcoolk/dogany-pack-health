# skincare-hygiene/kr-waived-dossier-active-list-013

Korea publishes a short closed list of actives and concentrations for which a
functional-cosmetic applicant does not have to file efficacy and safety data at
all. Understanding what that list is worth is the most useful regulatory fact in
this domain.

Whitening (the two statutory melanin functions):

- 닥나무추출물 2 percent
- 알부틴 2 to 5 percent
- 에칠아스코빌에텔 1 to 2 percent
- 유용성감초추출물 0.05 percent
- 아스코빌글루코사이드 2 percent
- 마그네슘아스코빌포스페이트 3 percent
- 나이아신아마이드 2 to 5 percent
- 알파-비사보롤 0.5 percent
- 아스코빌테트라이소팔미테이트 2 percent

Wrinkle improvement:

- 레티놀 2,500 IU per g
- 레티닐팔미테이트 10,000 IU per g
- 아데노신 0.04 percent
- 폴리에톡실레이티드레틴아마이드 0.05 to 0.2 percent

Acne-prone skin:

- 살리실릭애씨드 0.5 percent

Now read the annex's title. It is the list of functional cosmetics for which
DATA SUBMISSION IS OMITTED. Appearing on it means the applicant need not file
efficacy and safety substantiation. It does not mean the regulator examined the
product and concluded it worked. A product built from this list has had less
individual scrutiny than one that went through full review, not more. And the
notice states in its own reasons section that the latest amendment widened the
waiver to solid dosage forms specifically to support faster market entry. The
list is a throughput mechanism, described as one by the body that maintains it.

Three practical consequences.

The concentrations are fixed. A product using a listed active at some other
strength is outside the waiver, whatever the ingredient list suggests.

The waiver binds more than the ingredient. For whitening and wrinkle products the
dosage form is restricted to lotion, liquid, cream, solid and impregnated mask,
and both the permitted efficacy sentence and the directions for use are fixed
verbatim. For acne it is stricter still: the product must be a human-cleansing
product of soap composition, and the directions must say it is rinsed off
immediately with water. That is why a Korean acne functional cosmetic is by
construction a wash-off cleanser -- a fact about the shelf that no amount of
ingredient chemistry would predict.

And the list is short. Nine, four, one. Almost every ingredient sold as a hero
active in this category is not on it, which means the product either went through
individual review or is not making a registered functional claim at all.

One vocabulary trap. Whitening here means the two statutory melanin functions --
preventing deposition, lightening what is deposited. Radiance, glow, tone-up and
brightening in the marketing sense are not those functions and are not covered by
anything on this page.
