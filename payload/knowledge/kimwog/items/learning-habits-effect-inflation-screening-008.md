# learning-habits/effect-inflation-screening-008

A recent screening approach flags implausibly large effect sizes in the
published trials of psychological treatments -- about 5% of effects in the
sample it checked -- and
shows that excluding those flagged studies can meaningfully shrink pooled
meta-analytic estimates and reduce heterogeneity.

This is a preprint, not yet peer-reviewed, so carry it as a directional
finding rather than a fixed number. Note also how the tool works: it flags a
result only when an extreme effect, severe lack of power and high risk of bias
all occur together. A large effect on its own is not a flag, and the tool's
thresholds are calibrated to its own databases, so it should not be shipped
here as an operational screening rule.
