# learning-habits/replication-calibration-001

The single most important calibration fact for this whole domain: the original
Open Science Collaboration replication project rated only 39% of 100
psychology studies as replicated (36% reached statistical significance), with surviving effects roughly half their
original size. Pooling that project with later large-scale replication efforts
brings the rate up to about 64%, with effects still shrinking by roughly a
third.

Practical rule: any single, unreplicated psychology finding in this warehouse
should be discounted by default, not taken at face value.
