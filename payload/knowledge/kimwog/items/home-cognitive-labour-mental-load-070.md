refraction_notes:
  - note: >
      FOUR STAGES, NOT ONE TASK. Cognitive household labour is defined as
      anticipating a need, identifying options for meeting it, deciding among
      them, and monitoring the result. A chore list that records only who DID
      the visible step (bought the groceries, cleaned the bathroom) records
      the execution and none of the four stages around it.
    grade: C
  - note: >
      DECIDING IS THE SHARED PART; NOTICING AND CHECKING ARE NOT. In the
      interview samples women did more cognitive labour overall and more of
      the anticipation and monitoring in particular, while participation in
      decision-making was roughly equal. A couple that "decides together"
      can still have one partner carrying the whole front and back of every
      task. Ask who noticed and who follows up, not who chose.
    grade: C
  - note: >
      PHYSICAL EQUALITY DOES NOT IMPLY COGNITIVE EQUALITY. In the dissertation
      sample an egalitarian split was considerably more common in physical
      than in cognitive labour. A household whose diary hours look balanced
      may still be unbalanced on this dimension, and item 013's figures
      undercount the work for the same reason.
    grade: C
  - note: >
      FEELING SOLELY RESPONSIBLE TRACKS WORSE WELL-BEING, CROSS-SECTIONALLY.
      Among 393 partnered US mothers, feeling disproportionately responsible
      for household management was associated with strain on personal
      well-being and lower satisfaction with the relationship, after
      controlling for emotional and physical intimacy. One survey, one point
      in time, mostly upper-middle-class: an association, not a dose, and not
      evidence that moving the load fixes it.
    grade: C
  - note: >
      THE WORK IS OFTEN INVISIBLE TO THE PERSON DOING IT. Daminger's own
      future-research section says quantifying cognitive load is hard because
      much of it is invisible even to the laborer, and that survey instruments
      are still being developed. There is no validated score to compute and
      no threshold to compare one against. Do not build or quote one.
    grade: C
  - note: >
      WHAT A ROUTINE SURFACE CAN DO WITH THIS, AND ITS LIMIT. The one
      mechanical move the definition supports is to assign a recurring task
      end to end -- one owner for notice, options, decision and follow-up --
      rather than assigning only the execution step, and to let the system
      carry the anticipation (the reminder) and the monitoring (the check)
      that would otherwise sit in someone's head. That is a design inference
      from the definition. No trial shows it reduces anyone's load or
      improves anything.
    grade: D
  - note: >
      POPULATION. Different-sex couples in the US; the ASR study is
      college-educated, the dissertation adds working-class respondents and
      reports few differences by education. No Korean measurement of
      cognitive household labour was located (GAPS.md). Apply the structure,
      not the proportions, to anyone else, and never infer a specific
      person's share from their gender.
    grade: D
claim: >
  Household work has a cognitive dimension separate from the physical
  chores: anticipating what the household needs, identifying options,
  deciding, and monitoring the result. In US interview studies of
  different-sex couples, women carried most of it, especially the
  anticipation and monitoring, while decision-making was roughly shared, and
  an equal split was much more common in physical than in cognitive work.
  Because the work is largely invisible, even to the person doing it, time
  diaries and chore lists undercount it. In a cross-sectional survey of 393
  partnered mothers, feeling solely responsible for household management was
  associated with lower well-being and lower relationship satisfaction. For a
  routine or task surface the usable consequence is structural: a recurring
  task is four stages plus the execution, and assigning or recording only the
  execution misses the part that is unequally carried. No intervention that
  redistributes this load has been tested.
reasoning: >
  The definition is inductive, from 70 interviews in the published article
  and 136 in the full dissertation, and it is the most cited operational
  breakdown of what is popularly called mental load. Qualitative work earns
  the structure and the direction of the allocation, not a proportion: no
  share of cognitive labour is carried here as a number. Ciciolla and Luthar
  add an outcome link in a larger sample, but cross-sectionally and in a
  narrow class band, so the association stays observational and the
  direction of causation is open (a strained parent may also report feeling
  more solely responsible). Both sources were read at publisher-adjacent
  pages and the dissertation in full; the ASR article itself was read at
  abstract level, and its numbers match the dissertation's chapter. The
  single design move offered -- whole-task ownership with the system holding
  the reminders and checks -- is reasoned from the definition and is graded
  as such.

# home/cognitive-labour-mental-load-070 -- 집안일의 보이지 않는 절반: 알아채기·찾기·정하기·챙기기

**한 줄 그림:** 집안일에는 몸으로 하는 일 말고도 필요한 걸 알아채고, 방법을 찾고, 정하고, 잘
됐는지 챙기는 일이 따로 있다. 정하는 건 둘이 비슷하게 해도, 알아채고 챙기는 일은 한쪽에 몰리기
쉽다.

## What the engine reads from this row

| Stage | What it is | Who carried it in the samples | Strength |
|---|---|---|---|
| anticipate | noticing a need before it is urgent | mostly women | qualitative, consistent |
| identify | finding options | mostly women | qualitative |
| decide | choosing among options | roughly shared | qualitative |
| monitor | checking it got done and worked | mostly women | qualitative, consistent |
| execute | the physical chore (item 013 measures this) | more often shared than the stages above | qualitative + diary |
| outcome link | feeling solely responsible -> lower well-being and relationship satisfaction | mothers, upper-middle class | one cross-sectional survey |
| intervention | whole-task ownership; system holds reminders and checks | -- | design inference, untested |

## 한국어 요약 (답변용)

- 집안일은 몸으로 하는 일만이 아니다. 필요한 걸 미리 알아채고, 방법을 찾고, 정하고, 끝났는지
  챙기는 머리 쓰는 일이 따로 있다. 흔히 "멘탈 로드"라고 부르는 것이다.
- 미국 부부 인터뷰 연구에서는 정하는 일은 둘이 비슷하게 했지만, 알아채고 챙기는 일은 아내 쪽에
  훨씬 많이 몰려 있었다. 그래서 "같이 정한다"고 해도 앞뒤 일이 한 사람에게 쏠려 있을 수 있다.
- 몸으로 하는 일은 반반이어도 머리 쓰는 일은 반반이 아닌 경우가 많았다. 생활시간조사(013) 같은
  시간 기록에는 이 부분이 잡히지 않는다.
- 혼자 다 챙긴다고 느끼는 엄마들은 삶의 만족도와 관계 만족도가 낮았다. 다만 한 시점의 설문이라
  원인까지는 말할 수 없다.
- 점수나 기준선은 없다. 측정 도구가 아직 만들어지는 중이라 "당신의 멘탈 로드는 몇 점" 같은 말은
  하지 않는다. 성별로 특정 사람의 몫을 짐작하지도 않는다.
- 루틴에서 할 수 있는 건 하나다. 반복되는 일은 실행만 나누지 말고 처음(알아채기)부터 끝(챙기기)까지
  한 사람이 맡게 하고, 알림과 확인은 시스템이 들고 있게 한다. 이건 정의에서 끌어낸 설계 판단이지,
  효과가 검증된 방법은 아니다.
