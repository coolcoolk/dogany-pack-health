# skincare-hygiene/curator-checklist-015

Operating rules for this domain, distilled from the worked examples in it rather
than established independently.

The prior that drives everything else: this is the most commercially captured
subject matter in the warehouse. Most claims in circulation originate with
somebody selling the thing. That is not a reason to disbelieve everything. It is
a reason to require the provenance chain before believing anything.

**Clinical lane.** Top confidence needs randomised evidence pooled at
moderate-or-better certainty, an objective endpoint or a blinded assessor, and no
credible dispute. Solid confidence needs randomised or well-conducted
systematic-review evidence whose limits are stated and survivable -- the
Queensland sunscreen trial sits here, not higher, because it is one trial in one
town with no independent replication. The middle band is where consistent
direction across small heterogeneous trials with no pooled estimate lands. Below
that: practitioner assertion, uncontrolled observation, and searched absences.

**Regulatory lane.** A government instrument is definitive about what it says and
about nothing else. Quote it with its instrument number and effective date.
Re-check the edition before use -- everything cited in this lane was revised
within the last year. And never let it license a statement about efficacy.

**Downgrade or refuse when any of these is true:**

- the source is a party selling the product
- the endpoint is participant self-assessment, or an instrument reading with no
  blinded assessor
- there is no control arm
- the molecule or the concentration tested is not the one being sold
- the claim is being carried across a jurisdiction boundary without that
  jurisdiction's own instrument
- beauty media or a brand page is the only trace
- a patent, an ingredient list or a registration mark is offered in place of an
  outcome
- the full text was not retrievable, so the funding and risk-of-bias structure
  underneath a pooled estimate is unread

**Refusal is a valid output.** A famous claim declined with a stated reason is a
better result than a plausible claim shipped. And where a search comes back empty
on a question people actually ask, that absence ships as its own row, because
silence is where an unsourced number gets generated at answer time.

The characteristic error of this domain, worth naming on its own: reading a
registration mark, a waiver list or a permitted advertising claim as evidence
that something works. Those are facts about paperwork. They are useful, they are
verifiable, and they say nothing at all about outcomes.
