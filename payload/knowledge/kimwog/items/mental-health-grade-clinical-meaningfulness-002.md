# mental-health/grade-clinical-meaningfulness-002

Statistical significance alone never earns a top grade in this domain. The
GRADE-based standard additionally requires symptom AND functional improvement
holding at follow-up, low harm, reasonable resource use, real-world
effectiveness, and a plausible mechanism -- because pseudoscientific
interventions can and do produce statistically significant RCTs.

A result that is significant but not clinically meaningful should be graded
weak, not strong, regardless of the p-value.
