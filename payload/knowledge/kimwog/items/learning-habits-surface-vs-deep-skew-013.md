# learning-habits/surface-vs-deep-skew-013

The same Donoghue & Hattie (2021) meta-analysis that anchors
learning-habits/spaced-retrieval-meta-012 also breaks its pooled effects down
by outcome depth. Surface/factual-recall outcomes show a much larger average
effect (d=0.60) than deep/relational-learning outcomes (d=0.26) -- and the
deep-learning estimate rests on far fewer data points (N=109 vs N=1,473),
so it's also less precise.

Practical takeaway: the strong evidence behind retrieval practice and spaced
practice (-010, -012) is strongest for surface/factual learning. Don't
over-generalize it to claims about deeper conceptual or relational
understanding -- the evidence there is thinner and the effect noticeably
smaller.
