# exercise/deficit-volume-guidance-016

Common coaching advice says to keep training volume modest (toward the lower
end of the effective range) while eating in a calorie deficit, on the theory
that a body under-fed has less capacity to recover from hard training. That
reasoning is plausible, and the surrounding coaching consensus agrees with
it -- but the specific research claims that would have turned this into
verified science did not hold up: claims that a high or increasing set count
during a diet phase actually preserves more muscle than cutting volume back
were checked directly against the evidence and did not survive.

So this guidance should be delivered as sensible, widely-held practitioner
judgment, not as something a trial has proven. If a user is dieting, capping
volume moderately is a reasonable default to suggest -- just say plainly that
it rests on theory and coaching experience rather than a settled study.
