# exercise/replication-power-crisis-005

The exercise-science performance literature is dominated by small, underpowered
studies that over-report significant results. The practical consequence for a
curator is a hard rule: a single positive small-n finding in this field is not
trustworthy on its own and must be discounted until replicated.

This is a statement about the field's method, not about any one intervention.
One widely repeated shrinkage statistic (~50% mean shrinkage) did not survive
verification and should not be quoted; the verified figures are that 88% of
replicated effects shrink, with a median reduction of about 75%.
