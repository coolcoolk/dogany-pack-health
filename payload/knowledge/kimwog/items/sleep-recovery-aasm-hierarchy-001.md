# sleep-recovery/aasm-hierarchy-001

Sleep medicine has a genuine authoritative spine: the AASM sets standards and
accredits centers in the US, and its guidelines are built on systematic reviews
with explicit weighing of benefits and harms rather than expert opinion. That
process is what makes the field's top confidence band real.

The precise center count varies across sources and is not load-bearing; the
jurisdiction is the US, Canada, and territories. The point for a curator is that
an AASM systematic-review guideline is a high-certainty anchor.
