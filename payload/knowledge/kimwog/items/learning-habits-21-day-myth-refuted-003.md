# learning-habits/21-day-myth-refuted-003

The popular "21 days to form a habit" claim has no empirical basis -- it
traces to a 1960 anecdotal claim by Maxwell Maltz, not a study. The actual
meta-analytic median is 59-66 days, with a mean of 106-154 days and a very
wide individual range (4 to 335 days).

Refute the 21-day figure confidently. Carry the specific day-count numbers
with a caveat: only 4 of the 20 underlying studies measured duration directly,
and the rest rely on self-report scales, so the precise numbers are somewhat
less certain than the myth-refutation itself.
