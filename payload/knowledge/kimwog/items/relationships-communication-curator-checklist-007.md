# relationships-communication/curator-checklist-007

This is the operating checklist for grading anything new in this domain,
distilled from the confirmed worked examples above rather than a primary
finding in its own right: replicated consensus is genuinely rare here (top
grade), couples-therapy-style RCTs with sample-generalizability annotation sit
a notch below, observational correlations should be reported at their real
effect sizes (r=.20-.35, never headline percentages), and clinical/counseling-
practitioner consensus and pop self-help sit at the bottom.

Auto-downgrade triggers to watch for: suspiciously precise universal
constants, accuracy percentages with no base rate or PPV, forced-choice-
derived typologies, authority resting on book sales, statistics that survived
a formal retraction, and imported physics/math formalism with no domain
justification.
