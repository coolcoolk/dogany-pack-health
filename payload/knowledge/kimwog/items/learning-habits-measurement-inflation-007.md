# learning-habits/measurement-inflation-007

How practice/expertise is measured goes with a substantially different
apparent effect size across studies: retrospective self-report interviews find roughly 20% of
variance explained, questionnaires find about 15%, and objective practice logs
find only about 4% (questionnaire and log figures as corrected by the authors'
2018 corrigendum; the interview figure is the 2014 one).

Rule: when a claim rests on retrospective self-report measurement, downgrade
it toward the objective-log estimate rather than citing the self-report figure
at face value.
