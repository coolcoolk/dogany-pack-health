# learning-habits/measurement-inflation-007

How practice/expertise is measured changes the apparent effect size
substantially: retrospective self-report interviews find roughly 20% of
variance explained, questionnaires find about 12%, and objective practice logs
find only about 5%.

Rule: when a claim rests on retrospective self-report measurement, downgrade
it toward the objective-log estimate rather than citing the self-report figure
at face value.
