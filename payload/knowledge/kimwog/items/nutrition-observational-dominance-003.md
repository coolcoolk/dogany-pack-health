# nutrition/observational-dominance-003

Nutrition evidence is overwhelmingly observational, and the exposures are so
inter-correlated that confounding is nearly impossible to fully remove. The
practical rule for a curator is that a pile of agreeing observational studies is
not the same as a causal answer, and small relative risks (roughly 0.95-1.05)
should not be graded above the observational band.

This is a statement about how nutrition evidence behaves, not about any single
food. It is the methodological floor that the rest of the nutrition lane's
downgrade rules rest on.
