# learning-habits/learning-techniques-high-utility-010

Of the ten study techniques evaluated in the APS Dunlosky et al. (2013)
consensus review, two are rated HIGH utility: retrieval practice (testing
yourself) and distributed/spaced practice (spreading study sessions out over
time rather than massing them). The rating generalizes across ages, ability
levels, and subject areas -- this is the strongest evidence-backed
recommendation this domain has.

The strongest support is for surface/factual learning outcomes; the evidence
base is thinner for deeper, relational learning (see
learning-habits/surface-vs-deep-skew-013 for the scope caveat).
