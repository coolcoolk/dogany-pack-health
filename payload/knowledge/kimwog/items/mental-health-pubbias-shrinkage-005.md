# mental-health/pubbias-shrinkage-005

Publication bias inflates psychotherapy effect sizes more than the commonly-
quoted figure suggests. Bias-correction (trim-and-fill) across 117 depression
RCTs cut the mean effect size by roughly 37%, not the ~27-31% often cited
elsewhere -- and both standard publication-bias tests were highly significant.

Rule for this warehouse: an unadjusted meta-analytic effect size is not
sufficient grounds for a top grade on its own; look for a bias-adjusted or
preregistered-trial estimate before asserting a high-confidence effect size.
