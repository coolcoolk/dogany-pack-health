# sleep-recovery/grade-aasm-anchor-002

GRADE is the near-universal grading framework on the medical side, and the AASM
adopted it wholesale in 2016 with four certainty tiers. Its defining feature is
that it keeps certainty-of-evidence separate from strength-of-recommendation:
a strong recommendation can rest on weak evidence, and vice versa.

For a curator this is a discipline, not just a fact: the confidence letter grade
answers "how sure are we," which is a different question from "how strongly
should someone act." Keeping those two apart is exactly what GRADE enforces.
