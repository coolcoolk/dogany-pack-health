# exercise/set-counting-volume-unit-009

A weekly "set count" is a legitimate way to track training volume for muscle
growth, as long as those sets are taken to, or close to, muscular failure and
the rep range stays roughly between 6 and 20-plus. Outside that qualifier
(very low reps, sets stopped far from failure, or wildly different
conditions across comparisons), a raw set count stops being a fair apples-to-
apples volume unit.

This is the systematic-review-level backing behind the engine's basic move of
adding up weekly working sets per muscle rather than needing a more exotic
volume metric.
