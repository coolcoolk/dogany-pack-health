# relationships-communication/positivity-ratio-invalid-001

The widely-quoted "3-to-1 positivity ratio" is not a real finding. It traces
to Fredrickson & Losada (2005) borrowing Lorenz-system chaos parameters that
were never validated against human emotional data, and the exact figures
(2.9013, 11.6346) are artifacts of arbitrary illustrative constants, not
measurements. American Psychologist ran a formal correction in 2013.

This refutes the numeric ratio specifically. The looser intuition that
positive-to-negative interaction balance matters for relationships is a
separate, much weaker claim not covered by this item.
